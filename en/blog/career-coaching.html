Career coaching

What does career coaching look like — in Brno and online?

Mgr. Katka Hrouzková · 2026

Are you thinking about changing jobs, looking for a new professional direction, or feeling like you are not fully using your potential in your current role? Career coaching helps people better understand themselves, think through their options, and make decisions that are aligned with their values, strengths and life situation.

In my practice I work with clients from Brno and across the Czech Republic — both in person and online. Everyone comes with a different story, but the shared goal is usually greater clarity, confidence and satisfaction in their working life.

What can career coaching help with?

Career coaching is not only for people who want to hand in their notice. It often helps those who want to better understand their current situation and find their next step. Clients most commonly work on:

  • changing jobs or career direction
  • returning to work after parental leave
  • uncertainty about further professional development
  • long-term dissatisfaction at work
  • deciding between several job offers
  • developing management skills
  • preparing for a new role or greater responsibility
  • finding greater alignment between work, values and personal life

Many clients arrive with a feeling that "something is not right" but cannot yet name exactly what. Creating that clarity is often the first step towards change.

What does the first career consultation look like?

The first session is primarily about clarifying your situation and expectations. We talk about:

  • what has brought you here
  • what you would like to change
  • where you are right now
  • what an outcome you would be satisfied with would look like

Sometimes an important realisation or a new perspective on the situation emerges during the very first session. Other times we use it to set the direction for further work together.

How does career coaching work step by step?

Coaching is a conversation focused on finding your own answers and possibilities. I am not here to make decisions for you or to offer universal advice. During our sessions we work with things like:

  • questions that support deeper reflection
  • identifying strengths and resources
  • values and motivation
  • obstacles and limiting beliefs
  • concrete next steps and an action plan

Career coaching and working with talents

Sometimes a client knows they want a change but cannot name what direction would actually suit them. In these situations it can be useful to look at natural talents and ways of working.

Gallup CliftonStrengths

Gallup CliftonStrengths helps uncover natural talents and shows how to apply them at work and in everyday life. Clients often come away with a much clearer sense of what gives them energy and where they are naturally strong.

PRISM Brain Mapping

PRISM Brain Mapping is a methodology focused on understanding how we think, communicate and make decisions. It offers a practical lens on how we function — and on the differences between people.

Career coaching in Brno and online

Sessions take place in person in Brno or online via video call. The online format delivers the same quality of work, and many clients appreciate it for its flexibility and time efficiency.

What results can career coaching bring?

  • greater confidence in decision-making
  • a better understanding of your own strengths
  • increased self-confidence
  • a concrete plan of next steps
  • the courage to make a change that has been put off for a long time
  • greater satisfaction in working life

Career coaching is not about quick fixes or universal recipes. It is about finding solutions that will actually work for you.

Is career coaching right for you?

If you are facing an important decision, looking for a new professional direction, or want to get clearer on your options — career coaching can be a useful first step.

We will look at your situation together, go through your questions and work out what kind of support can best help you move forward.
