WHEN AN INTERNAL PERSPECTIVE IS NOT ENOUGH

  • Most companies have experience and the will to move things forward.
  • As the company grows, new processes, roles and expectations appear. Some practices stop being questioned.
  • What worked with ten people may not work with fifty.
  • In situations like these, a perspective from someone outside the day-to-day is often useful.
  • An external partner brings distance, experience from different environments, and questions that are rarely asked from within.
