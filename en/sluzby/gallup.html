Build on what comes naturally.

WHEN GALLUP CAN HELP YOU MOST

"I don't know what to build my career on."

Career development and self-knowledge

You will get clarity on what environment, role, and type of work give you the best chance to grow naturally. You will also better understand what comes easily to you, what motivates you, and where certain tasks drain more energy than they give.

"I want to understand why some things come naturally to me."

Self-knowledge and personal productivity

You will name your strengths and find out how to structure your work so it drains you less and draws more on what you naturally do well.

"I don't know how to divide work better within my team."

Team dynamics

You will name the talents in your team and distribute roles, responsibilities, and ways of working together more effectively.

"We often misunderstand each other about what people need and how they work."

Leadership and team dynamics

You will better understand differences in motivation, working style, and individual needs — and use that understanding in both leadership and day-to-day collaboration.

Why knowing your talent ranking is not enough

The report is a starting point, not an answer.

  • Two people can share the same top five talents and still operate completely differently.
  • The difference is shaped by experience, environment, role, and life circumstances.
  • That is why during the interpretation we do not look for generic definitions of talents. We look for how they show up specifically in you.
