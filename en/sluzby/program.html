Career Change Programme

You are not the same person you were ten years ago. Your career might not know that yet.

The programme brings together strengths-based work, HR experience, and a coaching approach. It is designed for the moment when your current direction stops making sense — even if everything looks fine from the outside.

When this kind of work makes the most sense

"After twenty years in the same field, I no longer feel it gives me anything."

Career change and sustainable ways of working

Your current work no longer reflects who you are today. Together we explore how to shape your work and life so they make sense in the long run.

"I am returning from parental leave and my priorities have completely changed."

A new chapter

Returning from parental leave, a change in life circumstances, or different priorities than before often opens the question of what actually makes sense in work right now — not just based on how things used to be.

"My work is changing and I don't know what comes next."

Uncertainty and change of direction

Your role, team, or entire environment is shifting and you are not sure what to hold on to. This work helps you rely more on yourself when making decisions, rather than on the expectations of others.

"I want a change, but I have no idea what kind."

Loss of direction

You know you need a change but you cannot yet see which way. We do not build an "ideal career plan" — we look for the next step that makes sense in your life as it is right now.

Why we do not start with a CV

  • Many career consultations begin with updating a CV or searching for suitable positions. That makes sense when you already know where you want to go.
  • But if you feel that your current direction has stopped making sense, it is often useful to pause one step earlier.
  • To look at your experience, talents, values, and motivation. At what is working in your work, what no longer is, and what you do not want to repeat.
  • Only then does the search for concrete options begin.

How it works

The programme typically runs over 5–7 sessions. The pace adapts to whether you need to first understand your situation or are ready to explore concrete options.
